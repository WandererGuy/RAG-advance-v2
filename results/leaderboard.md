## Reading this table

- **Every question was written by the agent** (ADR-0004). These numbers are fit for comparing pipelines against each other and for nothing else; retrieval metrics are inflated most, `refusal` is the least trustworthy of them.
- **The judge is the answering model** for: hybrid-v2, naive-v1 (ADR-0006). `faithful` and `relevance` are self-graded and biased upward; `refusal` and every retrieval column are deterministic and are not.
- Judge model(s): openai/gpt-5.6-luna.
- `cite ok` is the share of non-refusal answers carrying at least one citation; the per-run JSON also counts citations pointing at sources that were never retrieved.
- The corpus is 8 documents. Relative comparison between pipelines is valid; the absolute values are optimistic.
